Privacy

What we collect, and why
  • What we trackWe count each time a graphic is copied or downloaded on the Graphics Generator, specifically which template and style, which tab, whether it was copied or downloaded, the day, and the country it came from (plus the state in the US).
  • WhySeeing how much each design is used tells us which templates to improve, which to retire, and what we should make next.
  • What we don't keepYour IP address is only used to find the country and state, then dropped: it is never saved. We don't collect, and have no way of reading, player names, teams, event details, or anything you type or upload, besides links given to fetch tournament data from external sites. The Graphics Generator and Invitational standings don't use cookies; the Calendar site only uses them to keep you signed in and remember your last tab (its calendar view is an embedded Google Calendar, run by Google). We don't use outside tracking services. We cannot see the contents of files you upload, including images – only the relevant data is read from them, on your device, and then stored in your browser.
  • Who sees itOnly the Mismatched.gg admins. We use it to improve our most- and least-used templates, and to decide which languages to add next.
  • What you enter stays on your deviceEverything you enter in the Graphics Generator (players, teams, photos, branding) is saved in your own browser, not on our servers.
